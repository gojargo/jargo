# Code of Conduct

jargo follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in the project (in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other project space) is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexualized language or imagery.
- No publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, report it
privately to the maintainers. On the comment, issue or discussion concerned,
open the **...** menu, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository
admins**. The report reaches the maintainers only, not the person reported.

Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respond as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe: from a private warning
up to a permanent ban from the project's spaces, depending on the severity.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for those.
